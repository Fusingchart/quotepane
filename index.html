Questions

Where do the leads go?

To the email you enter in the builder, via FormSubmit. The very first request sends you a one-time "activate" email. Click it once and every lead after that arrives straight away.

Will it work on my Wix / Squarespace / WordPress / GoDaddy site?

Yes. Paste the one-line code into an "Embed code", "Custom HTML" or "Code" block. No website? Use the quote link instead.

Can I change my prices later?

Yes. The builder remembers your settings on your device. Edit, then re-paste the code or re-share the link.

Are the prices binding?

The widget calls it an estimate and says you'll confirm the final price. You can also show a ± range for extra room.

How do I unlock Pro?

Buy Pro, then paste the license key from your receipt email into the builder. Pro features switch on immediately.

What if it's not for me?

Email within 14 days for a full refund. No questions.
