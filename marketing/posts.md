## Reddit: r/sweatystartup (founders running service businesses)

**Title:** I built a free "instant quote" widget for pressure washing / window cleaning sites. Tell me what's missing

Most exterior-cleaning sites still say "call for a free estimate". The tools that let customers price the job themselves usually sit inside $50–180/month software plans, which is a lot when you're running one truck.

So I made a simple one:
- You set your rates (per sq ft, per window, per linear ft, flat) plus a minimum job charge
- Homeowner ticks services, types their square footage, and sees a price range right away
- They hit "book", and you get an email with name, phone, address and the breakdown
- Paste one line into WordPress/Wix/Squarespace. No website? There's a quote link + QR code for your Google profile, door hangers, etc.

Free for up to 3 services. Live demo on the page: https://fusingchart.github.io/quotepane/

Genuinely want to hear from people running these businesses: would you trust a customer-entered square footage, or do you always need to see the property first? What would stop you using this?
