# Gumroad product setup (5 minutes)

1. gumroad.com → New product → **Digital product**.
2. **Name:** QuotePane Pro: Instant-Quote Widget for Service Businesses
3. **Price:** $29 (untick "pay what you want")
4. **URL slug:** `quotepane`
5. **Content:** add a link button to https://fusingchart.github.io/quotepane/builder.html, labelled "Open the builder"
6. **Settings → License keys:** turn ON "Generate a unique license key per sale".
7. **Cover:** upload `marketing/cover.png`. **Thumbnail:** `marketing/thumb.png`.
8. **Publish.** Then send me:
   - the product URL (e.g. https://yourname.gumroad.com/l/quotepane)
   - the **product ID** (Product edit page → Content/License key section shows `product_id`)
   I'll put both in `config.js` so the Buy buttons and license unlock work.

## Description (paste)

**Stop driving out to quote.** QuotePane puts an instant-quote form on your website, or behind a link on your Google Business Profile, Facebook or Instagram. Homeowners pick services, enter their size and see a price. You get the lead in your inbox with their name, phone, address and full breakdown.

Built for pressure washing, soft washing, window cleaning, gutter cleaning, holiday light installs, house cleaning and lawn care. It works for any service you price per sq ft, per window, per linear foot, per item, per hour or flat.

**Pro unlocks:**
- Unlimited services (free plan: 3)
- No "QuotePane" branding
- Multi-service bundle discounts (e.g. 10% off 2+ services)
- Your own thank-you message
- All future updates

**Also included in the free plan:** website embed (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy), shareable quote link, printable QR code for flyers and door hangers, minimum job charge, per-service minimums, ± price range.

One payment. No monthly fee. No login. 14-day money-back guarantee.

Try the live demo first: https://fusingchart.github.io/quotepane/
